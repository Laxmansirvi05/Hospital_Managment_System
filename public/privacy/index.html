Privacy policy

CareRoute helps you find hospitals, doctors, medicines, pharmacies and diagnostic centres in Hyderabad, and keeps your medical ID, reminders, appointments and documents in one place. This page explains what we collect, why, where it is kept, who it is shared with, and how you can delete it.

In an emergency

Call 112. You do not need to sign in to CareRoute to call for help.

What we collect, and why

  • Your account: name, email address and phone number, so you can sign in and so we can show your details back to you. Your password is stored only as a scrambled hash that nobody, including us, can read back.
  • Your medical ID: the details you choose to enter, such as date of birth, blood group, allergies, conditions, current medicines, address, insurance and your own doctor. It is used to show your medical ID to you. It is shown to an ambulance crew only if you turn on sharing, and ambulance requests are not live yet.
  • Emergency contacts: the names and phone numbers you add. We do not message them today.
  • Medicine reminders, dose records and appointments: what you add, so we can remind you and show your day.
  • Documents and your photo: files you upload, kept private to you.
  • Reviews: ratings and text you post about a hospital, doctor, pharmacy or diagnostic centre. Other signed-in users see your first name only.
  • Sign-in sessions: two cookies (cr_access and cr_refresh) that keep you signed in. They are needed for the service to work, and we use no other cookies.
  • Abuse protection: counters of recent requests, stored as scrambled values rather than your IP address or email in plain text, to stop repeated sign-in attempts and heavy use.

Your location

If you choose "Use my location", your browser shares your position so results can be sorted by distance. It is sent with the search and is not stored on our servers. Your browser keeps it for the current session only. You can always search by area instead.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.
  • We use no analytics or tracking tools.
  • We do not take bookings, orders or payments. Links to hospitals and pharmacies open their own websites.
  • No AI service receives your data today. Before the AI assistant goes live, this page will say exactly what is sent and to whom.

Where your data is kept

Your account and health records are stored with Supabase, in its Mumbai region in India. The website and its server run on Vercel, also in Mumbai. Access to the database is limited to the CareRoute server, and every table is closed to direct access from browsers.

Who else can see something

  • Hospital websites, when you view doctor photos. Doctor photos load from each hospital's own website. That site can see your IP address and which photo was loaded. CareRoute's own page address is not sent.
  • Sites you choose to visit. When you follow a link to a hospital, pharmacy, diagnostic centre or Google Maps, that site receives what your browser normally sends. We add no tracking to these links.
  • Ambulance crews, once ambulance requests are live: your medical ID only if you turned on sharing, and your documents only after a booking is confirmed.

Listing and doctor information

Hospital, clinic, pharmacy and diagnostic centre listings come from OpenStreetMap and from providers' own websites. Doctor profiles come from the doctor pages that hospitals publish themselves. We show only professional facts the hospital publishes: name, title, speciality, qualifications, experience, languages, branch, the hospital's appointment number, its booking page, and a photo shown from the hospital's website. We never show a doctor's personal phone number or email. A doctor or hospital can ask us to remove a profile, and we remove it within 7 days.

How long we keep it

Your account data is kept until you delete your account. Retention periods for other records will be published here before launch.

Your choices and rights

  • See and correct your details on the Medical ID, Reminders, Appointments and Documents pages.
  • Turn medical ID sharing on or off at any time on the Medical ID page.
  • Change your password, or sign out of every other device, on the Account and privacy page.
  • Delete your account on the Account and privacy page. This removes your profile, medical ID, contacts, reminders, appointments, documents, photo and reviews straight away.

Contact

A contact address for privacy requests will be published here before public launch. Until then, signed-in users can delete their own data at any time from the Account and privacy page.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Important changes will be shown in the app before they take effect.
