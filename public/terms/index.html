Terms of use

By creating an account or using CareRoute, you agree to these terms. Please read them with the privacy policy.

CareRoute is not an emergency service

If someone may be seriously ill or injured, call 112 straight away. CareRoute does not dispatch ambulances today, and nothing in the app replaces calling for help.

CareRoute is not medical advice

CareRoute shows information that hospitals, pharmacies, diagnostic centres and public sources publish. It does not diagnose, recommend a doctor, or tell you which medicine to take. Medicines in the same group are not interchangeable without a doctor's advice. Always ask a doctor or pharmacist about your own care.

Where the information comes from

  • Places: OpenStreetMap, available under the Open Database License. Listing data © OpenStreetMap contributors.
  • Doctors: the doctor pages that each hospital publishes on its own website.
  • Medicine prices: the Jan Aushadhi product and MRP list published by the Government of India.
  • Test prices and diagnostic centres: what each diagnostic chain publishes on its own website.

Every listing shows its source and the date it was checked. Information can change after that date, so confirm timings, fees, prices and availability with the provider before you go.

Bookings, orders and payments

CareRoute does not take bookings, sell medicines or take payments. Booking buttons and phone numbers connect you to the hospital or provider directly, and their own terms apply there.

Your account

  • Give accurate details and keep your password to yourself.
  • You are responsible for what is done with your account. If you think someone else has used it, change your password and sign out other devices on the Account and privacy page.
  • You can delete your account at any time. Deleting it removes your data as the privacy policy describes.

Reviews

  • Review only places and doctors you have visited, and describe your own experience.
  • Do not include medical details about yourself or anyone else, phone numbers, or anything abusive.
  • Reviews that several people report are hidden until they are checked. We may remove reviews that break these rules.

Fair use

Do not try to break into accounts, overload the service, copy the listings in bulk, or get around its limits. We may suspend accounts that do.

Changes and availability

CareRoute is in development, and features may change or be unavailable at times. If these terms change, the date at the top changes too, and important changes are shown in the app before they take effect.

Limits on responsibility

To the extent the law allows, CareRoute is not responsible for decisions made using information from third-party sources, or for the services of hospitals, doctors, pharmacies and diagnostic centres you contact through it.
